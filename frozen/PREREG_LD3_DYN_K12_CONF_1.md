## Analysis rules (fixed before running)

1. Per-seed episode metrics: worst / weak-3 / steady P_D from the paired eval.
2. Three-gate pass: worst >= 0.60 AND weak3 >= 0.70 AND steady >= 0.80 per seed.
3. Report means, minima, 5th percentiles, and the pass count / 25 with a
   Wilson 95% lower bound for the pass fraction.
4. Paired same-seed deltas (candidate minus baseline) for worst/weak3/steady.
5. Safety and protocol invariants: continuous separation violation rate == 0,
   per-class deadline violation rate <= 0.05, serialized protocol deadline
   violation reported (expected to hold by design of the MAC accounting).

## Interpretation

- Candidate passes if its three-gate pass count is strictly greater than the
  baseline's AND the paired worst delta mean is positive with a positive
  paired-episode-bootstrap 95% lower bound.
- Otherwise the freeze target is the pre-fix engineering candidate and the new
  mechanisms stay default-off with the causal evidence reported.

## Amendment 3 (three-phase policy: gap coverage + product-order pursuit +
## deficit reinforcement; sealed before the runs below)

Purpose: fix the deep-audit root cause R1 (missed targets from local-view
stitching, agreement 0.72) with a deterministic gap-coverage movement
(`distributed_gap_coverage_movement_enabled`; design in
`docs/THREE_PHASE_POLICY_DESIGN_2026-08-25.md`):

- Phase A: every target keeps a Tx and an Rx responsibility (coverage
  invariant C1, critical radius 320 m); uncovered targets are re-covered
  every frame (safety net, no 150-frame hold).  Pursuit is product-order:
  chase argmax R_kq^2 * min_opposite R_jq^2 (steepest descent on the P_D-gate
  bottleneck) until the standoff clamp.
- Phase B: assignment ordered by local target deficit (1 - P_D) so weak
  targets receive endpoints first (`distributed_gap_deficit_priority`).
- Phase C: radius-preserving tangential step at the standoff boundary
  (`distributed_gap_tangential_phase`, default off until validated).

## Amendment 1 (hybrid free-ride top-up schedule, same analysis rules)

The candidate lost the paired gate with high variance (4 strong-seed
regressions) and degraded evidence delivery (0.9997 -> 0.948).  Hypothesis:
the decoupled schedule grew per-sender payloads / active senders past the
4 ms per-class deadline.  Amendment: the freshness scheduler now emits only
TOP-UP entries disjoint from the evidence selection (evidence entries
free-ride the posterior as in the legacy coupled schedule), with a per-source
union cap (`u2u_belief_feedback_max_union_per_source = 2`) and the same
400-bit global budget.  The analysis rules, seed set, and verdict rule are
unchanged; the candidate config is still
`exp_800_k12q12_distributed_v2_dynamic_u2u_robustbelief_freshness_pilot.yaml`
(now with the hybrid schedule).  If it passes, the freeze target is updated
to the hybrid candidate with the same snapshot/hash procedure.
